How every column is calculated

Sorted by Validated first, then MAR (return ÷ drawdown) within each group -- a strategy that fails even one of the five checks under Validated ranks below every strategy that passes all of them, whatever its other numbers say; see the banner above and click any column to sort by it instead. Captured is the share of the rule's own signals the account could afford; when it is low you are reading a cash constraint as much as a rule. Ulcer, Sharpe and Sortino move to the Detail view, where there is room to explain what each adds over MAR. Click a row for its detail.
