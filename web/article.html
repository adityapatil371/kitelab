Stock Analysis

This workbench tests rules-based swing trading on roughly a thousand Indian stocks — buy this, sell that, decided in advance by a written rule and followed without judgement, holding positions for days or weeks rather than for minutes or for years. We wrote three dozen such rules and ran each one hundreds of ways, simulated day by day against real prices reaching back two decades, with brokerage, taxes and the cost of moving the market itself all charged against every trade.

Every rule is measured against the laziest strategy there is: buy every stock in the list on the first day, never sell, and do nothing at all thereafter. This page calls that buy & hold.

So — did any of it beat buy & hold?
